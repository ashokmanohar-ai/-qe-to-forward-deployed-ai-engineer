# Portfolio projects

Build for evidence, not feature count. The strongest portfolio shows a coherent customer problem, explicit decisions, reproducible code, evaluation, security, deployment, observability, and operational ownership.

## Portfolio evidence rubric

Score each 0-4:

- **Problem/value:** baseline, users, workflow, measurable outcome, and non-goals.
- **Engineering:** structure, types, contracts, tests, errors, persistence, and maintainability.
- **AI quality:** datasets, metrics, segmentation, traces, failure taxonomy, and release gates.
- **Security:** identity, authorization, tenant/data boundaries, threats, secrets, and audit.
- **Deployment:** reproducible container/IaC, CI/CD, configuration, rollout, and rollback.
- **Operations:** SLOs, dashboards, alerts, performance/cost, runbooks, and incident drill.
- **Communication:** concise README, diagrams, ADRs, demo, limitations, and customer explanation.

Target at least 21/28, with no 0 in security or operations, before pinning a project on your profile.

## Case-study format

1. Customer context and measurable problem.
2. Constraints and rejected alternatives.
3. Architecture and critical decisions.
4. What you built and how to reproduce it.
5. Evaluation, security, reliability, performance, and cost evidence.
6. Failure discovered and how it changed the design.
7. Production gaps and next decision.
8. Your role, learning, and business outcome.
