# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive learning environment.

Be constructive, assume good intent, critique ideas rather than people, protect confidential information, and make room for learners with different backgrounds. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberate disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion. Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
